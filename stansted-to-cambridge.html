Stansted Airport → Cambridge

Stansted to Cambridge: direct train, coach, taxi & private transfer

Stansted Airport is one of the easiest London airports for reaching Cambridge because both train and coach can run direct. The best choice depends less on changing trains and more on where you actually need to finish: Cambridge railway station, Parkside, the historic city centre, a university college, Trumpington or an address outside central Cambridge.

Quick answer

What is the best way from Stansted Airport to Cambridge?

For most travellers going near Cambridge railway station, check the direct train first: it is usually the fastest public option. If you are going to Parkside, the historic centre or Trumpington, compare the direct National Express coach because its Cambridge stops may reduce your last-mile journey. Families, larger groups and travellers with several suitcases should also compare one fixed private-transfer quote.

Direct train

Stansted Airport to Cambridge train: usually the fastest public option

Greater Anglia operates direct trains between Stansted Airport and Cambridge. The operator currently lists up to two trains per hour on weekdays and a fastest journey of 29 minutes.

The current route page advertises fares from £9.50 each way, based on a return fare. Ticket prices can vary by time, ticket type and Railcard, so treat that as a live from-price rather than a guaranteed fare for every departure.

On weekdays, Greater Anglia currently lists the first Stansted Airport → Cambridge train at 05:20 and the last at 23:58. Weekend times can differ, so use the live journey planner for your actual travel date.

The big advantage is reliability of journey time: the train avoids motorway traffic and takes you directly to Cambridge railway station.

Important last-mile detail

Cambridge railway station is not in the middle of the historic centre. Greater Anglia describes it as roughly a mile from central Cambridge, around a 20–25 minute walk, with buses and a taxi rank available outside.

Direct coach

Stansted to Cambridge coach: useful for Parkside and Trumpington

National Express runs direct coaches from Stansted Airport to Cambridge. Its current Stansted → Cambridge page lists routes 727, 728 and 777, with services calling at Cambridge Parkside and, on 727/728 services, Trumpington Park & Ride.

The operator currently advertises its fastest Stansted to Cambridge coach at 45 minutes, with one-way fares from £9.80 subject to availability and fare conditions.

The direct coach can be more useful than the train when your destination is closer to Parkside, the historic centre or Trumpington. It also avoids carrying luggage through a railway station and can offer useful departure times outside the core rail pattern.

National Express currently lists the first daily Stansted → Cambridge coach at around 01:00 and the last at around 22:50. Always confirm the live timetable because route numbers, stopping patterns and times can change.

Choose the Cambridge stop carefully

“Cambridge” is not one single arrival point. Parkside is closer to the city centre; Trumpington Park & Ride is south of Cambridge. Check your hotel, college or postcode before booking.

Where are you actually going?

Cambridge Station, Parkside, city centre or university college?

Your final address can matter more than a 10–15 minute difference in the airport leg.

Cambridge railway station

The direct train is the obvious first comparison. You arrive at the station without a road-traffic risk or coach stop decision.

Historic city centre

Parkside can be more convenient than the railway station for parts of central Cambridge. Compare walking time from each arrival point to your exact address.

University colleges

Many colleges are spread across the centre. Neither “Cambridge station” nor “Parkside” is automatically closest to every college, so check the last mile.

Trumpington

A National Express service that calls at Trumpington Park & Ride may be much better than travelling into central Cambridge and back south again.

Addenbrooke’s / biomedical campus

The railway station may still require an onward bus or taxi. A private transfer becomes more useful if you have luggage or mobility needs.

Outer Cambridge / family address

If public transport still leaves a significant onward journey, compare the total group cost against a fixed door-to-door transfer.

Airport access

Finding the train or coach at Stansted Airport

Train

Stansted Airport has its own railway station directly connected to the terminal area. Follow airport signs for trains and check the platform for the direct Cambridge service rather than boarding a London-bound train automatically.

Coach

Stansted Airport’s coach station is opposite the main terminal entrance and the airport describes it as around a two-minute walk. National Express advises passengers to arrive before departure and check that the ticket matches the intended service.

Avoid these mistakes

Common Stansted to Cambridge planning mistakes

Assuming every airport train goes to Cambridge

Stansted also has frequent London services. Check the departure board for the direct Cambridge train rather than following the busiest crowd automatically.

Treating Cambridge Station as “city centre”

The railway station is around a mile from the historic centre. Add the walk, local bus or taxi when comparing the train with a Parkside coach.

Booking the wrong coach stop

Parkside and Trumpington serve different parts of Cambridge. Choosing the right stop can save a second bus or taxi.

Booking too close to your landing time

Your aircraft landing time is not your train or coach departure time. Allow for passport control, baggage reclaim and the walk from arrivals.

Frequently asked questions

Stansted Airport to Cambridge FAQ

Is there a direct train from Stansted Airport to Cambridge?

Yes. Greater Anglia runs direct services, currently up to two trains per hour on weekdays.

How fast is the Stansted to Cambridge train?

Greater Anglia currently lists the fastest direct journey at 29 minutes. Check the live departure because not every train has the same journey time.

How much is the Stansted to Cambridge train?

Greater Anglia currently advertises fares from £9.50 each way based on a return fare. Your live price can differ by ticket type and travel time.

Is there a direct coach from Stansted Airport to Cambridge?

Yes. National Express operates direct services to Cambridge, including Parkside and, on some routes, Trumpington Park & Ride.

How long does the Stansted to Cambridge coach take?

National Express currently advertises its fastest journey at 45 minutes, although traffic and stopping pattern can change the real time.

Which is better: train or coach?

Train is generally best for speed and Cambridge railway station. Coach can be better for Parkside, Trumpington, luggage or a departure time that fits your flight more closely.
