Choose by your situation

The cheapest ticket is not always the simplest journey

Airport transfer decisions change with luggage, group size, arrival time and where you are really staying.

🧳

Heavy luggage

Count the changes, stairs and final walk — not only the headline fare. A direct coach or door-to-door transfer can be easier than a multi-change rail route.

🌙

Late-night arrival

Check the last train before choosing rail. Some coach routes run much later or around the clock; private transfers can cover gaps when public transport is limited.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family or group

Compare the total group cost. Four separate rail or coach tickets can narrow the gap with a fixed-price private vehicle.

🎯

Not staying in central London

Avoid the automatic London detour. Routes such as Heathrow → Oxford and Gatwick → Brighton can be served directly without crossing central London first.

Airport transfer FAQ

Questions travellers ask before leaving the terminal

What does Terminal2City compare?

We compare practical ways to get from an airport to a city or final destination: train, coach and private transfer options where they make sense for that route.

Does Terminal2City only cover airport transfers to London?

No. A core idea of the site is to help travellers who are not actually going to central London. Heathrow to Oxford and Gatwick to Brighton are examples of routes where a direct onward journey can avoid an unnecessary London detour.

Are journey times and prices guaranteed?

No. Traffic, engineering work, timetable changes and dynamic fares can all change the final journey. We show route mechanics and checked reference information, then point you to live operator information before booking.
