How long does it take to leave Heathrow Airport after landing?

Landing is not the same as reaching the arrivals hall. Find out what happens between touchdown and the exit at Heathrow Terminals 2, 3, 4 and 5 — and estimate a sensible pickup or train time based on your own circumstances.

Quick answer

Allow time for the whole arrivals process, not just the flight

There is no guaranteed time to leave Heathrow after landing. As an illustrative planning exercise, a straightforward arrival with hand luggage could take around 45–75 minutes from touchdown to the public arrivals hall; arriving with checked bags or a slow border queue can take longer, and substantial delays can push the wait beyond two hours. These are not measured Heathrow averages or service guarantees. Build your plan around the flight, your documents, luggage and final transport meeting point.

Heathrow landing-to-exit time: useful planning scenarios

Searches for “how long to get out of Heathrow” and “Heathrow arrivals time after landing” often return a single number. That can be misleading. A traveller with an eligible biometric passport and hand luggage faces a different sequence from a family collecting a pushchair and three suitcases.

How long does passport control take at Heathrow?

Heathrow immigration waiting times depend on arriving aircraft, staffing, passport eligibility and whether additional checks are necessary. Even if an eGate is open, a passenger can be directed to an officer. Heathrow and Border Force do not promise an individual passenger a fixed border clearance time. A flight delay can also change which arrival wave your flight joins.

Who can use Heathrow eGates in 2026?

According to the UK government, eligible biometric-passport holders can use eGates. Since 8 July 2026, qualifying children aged 8 and 9 have also been eligible, alongside older children: those aged 8–17 must be accompanied by an adult and be at least 120 cm tall. Nationality, valid permission to enter the UK and document requirements still apply. Under-8s cannot use the eGates. Do not assume that every traveller can use them just because their passport is biometric.

For a smoother arrival, have your passport and immigration permission ready, remove the passport cover, and put away your phone, sunglasses and headphones when approaching the check. Travelling as a family? Stay together and follow the staff's lane instructions. Current rules are on the UK government border control page.

Does Heathrow offer arrivals Fast Track?

Yes. Heathrow advertises an optional Fast Track Arrivals service for dedicated passport-control lanes, subject to eligibility, availability and the booked slot. It is not a promise of instant entry, and it cannot speed up a delayed aircraft, a late baggage belt or the journey from the arrivals hall to the station. Check the official Heathrow Fast Track page for availability and current terms; this article does not include changing prices.

How long does baggage reclaim take at Heathrow?

Heathrow's official arrivals process places baggage reclaim after passport control. In the reclaim hall, check the display for your flight number and belt. Free baggage trolleys are available. Bags can appear promptly, but how long you personally wait depends on baggage handling, the aircraft's parking position and whether your suitcase is among the first or last unloaded.

If your bag is missing or damaged, contact your airline's baggage service before leaving the reclaim area where possible; this may delay your transfer pickup further. Heathrow itself directs passengers to airline staff for baggage-delivery enquiries. See Heathrow baggage reclaim guidance.

Cabin bag vs checked suitcase

With cabin baggage only, enter 0 minutes in the calculator's baggage step and plan a reasonable walk through customs. With checked luggage, do not subtract the baggage stage simply because the plane landed on time. Families collecting pushchairs, oversized luggage or sports equipment may need to allow additional time.

Does Heathrow Terminal 2, 3, 4 or 5 change the exit time?

Terminal layout changes your walk to the train, bus, car or pickup point more than it can predict your immigration wait. Aircraft may arrive at remote stands or different gates, so even two flights into the same terminal can have different walks.

For Heathrow Express, use Heathrow's official operator information. Bus stops and private hire meeting points are separate: always follow your actual operator's instructions.

How long after landing should you book a Heathrow taxi, train or coach?

Meeting a private airport transfer driver

Never treat the touchdown time as the moment you can shake hands with the driver. Some firms track your flight number and include a waiting allowance; others start charging at a stated time, limit the free wait or declare a no-show. Before booking, confirm whether waiting starts from scheduled landing, actual landing or a specified pickup time, and whether you meet in the terminal or at a car park. If you have checked bags or may need extra immigration checks, share that detail with your provider.

Taking Heathrow Express, the Elizabeth line or the Tube

A train departure time is not your exit time. First reach the public arrivals hall, then find the correct station and platform. If you're heading towards Paddington, consider whether Heathrow Express or the Elizabeth line better suits your ticket flexibility and final destination. To compare the journey beyond the airport rather than just the rail leg, see our Heathrow to London transport guide.

Booking a coach with a fixed departure

Leave enough time not only to exit the terminal but also to reach the departure stop and board before any cutoff. Check if your coach ticket has an add-on that allows another departure after a flight delay. If a late arrival already made you miss your booking, read our missed airport transfer after a delayed flight guide before paying for a replacement ticket.

How to reduce unnecessary delays at Heathrow arrivals

  • Check your terminal. The flight's arrival terminal and your bus or train departure point are separate pieces of information.
  • Know whether you may use an eGate. Carry the right documents, but expect that a Border Force officer can still be required.
  • Keep essentials in cabin luggage. Your passport, digital permission details and transfer booking should be easy to reach.
  • Look up the checked-bag belt. Heathrow displays baggage belt numbers by arriving flight; contact the airline for missing bags.
  • Allow extra time when travelling with children or assistance needs. If you have pre-booked special assistance, the airport may support you through arrivals and onward to transport.
  • Check the last train and your actual destination. Landing before midnight does not guarantee you'll catch a late service once you've exited and walked to the platform.

Travellers who need assistance can consult Heathrow's assistance arrival guide. For independently booked connecting flights, remember that entering the UK, reclaiming bags, changing terminals and checking in again is a much longer process than just leaving arrivals. See Heathrow's self-connection guidance.

Frequently asked questions about Heathrow arrivals

How long does it take to get out of Heathrow Airport after landing?

There is no reliable fixed time. Passengers must allow for disembarkation, walking, immigration where required, baggage reclaim and customs. The example ranges in this guide are planning scenarios, not Heathrow-published average wait times.

Is 60 minutes enough to leave Heathrow after landing?

It may be enough if the flight docks promptly, border control is quick and you have no checked baggage, but it is not guaranteed. Travellers collecting bags or requiring additional checks should allow more flexibility.

How long does passport control take at Heathrow?

There is no guaranteed queue time. Waits depend on arrivals, passport eligibility, staffing and any extra checks. Eligible passengers may use eGates.

Can children use Heathrow eGates in 2026?

Eligible children aged 8 to 17 may use UK eGates if at least 120 cm tall and accompanied by an adult. Under-8s cannot use them. Passport and immigration eligibility requirements also apply.

How long after landing should I book a Heathrow airport taxi?

Follow your taxi operator's rules for flight tracking, pickup time and waiting allowance. Allow for immigration, baggage and the walk to its meeting point rather than using the scheduled landing time as the exact pickup time.

How long from Heathrow arrivals to the Elizabeth line station?

The walk depends on the terminal and where you exit arrivals. Terminals 2 and 3 share a station reached by a pedestrian subway, while Terminal 4 has a station below arrivals and Terminal 5 has one in the terminal basement. No guaranteed walking duration applies to every traveller.
