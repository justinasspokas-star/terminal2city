Stansted Airport early morning flights: how to get there for a 5am or 6am departure

For an early flight, the question isn't simply “When does the first train run?” It's “Will I reach check-in or security early enough?” Compare overnight coaches, the first Stansted Express, taxis and airport hotels — and work backwards from your departure time.

The short answer

For a 5am or 6am Stansted flight, start with a night coach, taxi or airport hotel — not the first regular train

Stansted advises passengers to arrive at least two hours before short-haul flights, while some airlines require three or even four hours. Its published first regular Stansted Express services from Liverpool Street do not reach the airport early enough for a two-hour check-in target before a 05:00 or 06:00 departure. National Express advertises overnight London–Stansted coaches; you can also compare a car or hotel close to the terminal. Always verify your airline's check-in deadline and your exact service for the travel date.

What time should you reach Stansted for a 5am or 6am flight?

There is a crucial distinction between flight departure time, terminal arrival time and when your transport must leave London. Stansted's current check-in guidance distinguishes airlines, with three hours for international Ryanair flights and two hours for its domestic flights, for example. It also recommends validating your boarding pass at security at least two hours before the scheduled departure. Always use the requirement that applies to your specific flight.

Why this matters for a 06:00 flight

On Monday, Friday or Saturday, a normal 03:40 first train is only expected to reach Stansted around 04:30. That's later than a 04:00 two-hour target — before allowing for the walk into the terminal. Tuesday–Thursday's first regular train is later still. “There's an early train” is not the same as “it gets me there in time”.

Night bus or coach to Stansted Airport: what to check

National Express operates London–Stansted airport coaches around the clock, with routes and stopping points that include central and east London. A night coach can be much better suited to a 05:00 departure than the first standard rail service. However, the last mile to your London coach stop also matters: the Underground or a local connecting train may not operate when you need it.

Use National Express's dated London–Stansted journey planner and select the arrival at Stansted Airport, rather than simply booking the earliest departure you can find. Services, stop locations, change conditions and journey time vary. Coach timetables may differ on bank holidays and can be affected by roadworks, motorway incidents and traffic.

  1. Find the closest operating night-time pickup point, not just the closest daytime bus stop.
  2. Choose a coach scheduled to reach Stansted ahead of your airline's check-in or security target.
  3. Add a reasonable allowance for walking, baggage and road disruption. A very tight margin defeats the advantage of booking ahead.
  4. Look up the ticket's change conditions and save the coach company's contact details.
  5. Check how you will get to the departure stop at 01:00 or 02:00. You might need a short taxi to the coach stop.

Published examples and exact A6/A8 service times can change, so the operator's current route timetable is a better source than an undated blog schedule. A return trip from Stansted back to London has different first/last rail timings; see our separate Stansted to London route comparison.

Taxi or private transfer for a 5am Stansted departure

A licensed taxi or pre-booked private vehicle can remove the problem of finding a coach stop during the night. It may also become more competitive for three or four travellers sharing one price, particularly with children or several bags. But a vehicle is not automatically more reliable than a scheduled coach; you still face possible roadworks, congestion and driver no-shows.

Ask the operator for the complete vehicle price from your address to Stansted, any overnight surcharge, luggage allowance, child-seat arrangements and what happens if the driver is late. Confirm the pickup location and time in writing. Compare one full vehicle quote against the combined cost of each coach fare plus any overnight journey to the stop, rather than comparing the headline coach fare with a taxi price.

Worked decision: family of four

If your family is staying far from an operating night-coach stop, a taxi to that stop plus four coach seats can change the total significantly. There is no universal winner: obtain a current quote and compare the final door-to-terminal cost and arrival time before booking.

Airport hotel before an early flight: sometimes the best value is sleep

When a 05:00 or 06:00 flight means leaving accommodation in the middle of the night, staying near Stansted can simplify the journey. Two examples listed by the airport are Radisson Blu London Stansted Airport (about a two-minute walk via its terminal link) and Hampton by Hilton London Stansted Airport (about a five-minute walk through a covered walkway). Check rates and room availability for your specific night — there is no reliable fixed hotel price.

Not all airport hotels are walkable: others need a shuttle, and the first shuttle may affect an early check-in. Before booking, verify the path to the terminal, shuttle hours, family-room capacity and whether your ticket or hotel allows cancellation.

Can you spend the night inside Stansted Airport?

Do not rely on sleeping in the terminal. Stansted says the airport operates 24/7, but its passenger FAQ states that the terminal closes between midnight and 02:00, and that passengers departing on early flights should not arrive before their scheduled check-in time. The airport also does not permit bedding down in the terminal. This is especially important for travellers considering taking a very late coach and waiting for a 05:00 flight.

Read the airport's current overnight terminal guidance and your airline's check-in opening time. A hotel or a properly timed night coach may be more practical than deliberately arriving at 23:00 for a flight several hours later.

Do these plans change for Ryanair, easyJet or checked baggage?

Yes. Stansted's airport guidance currently lists Ryanair international flights: 3 hours, Ryanair domestic and easyJet: 2 hours, and Jet2, TUI and AJet: 3 hours for recommended pre-flight arrival. Emirates is listed at 4 hours. These are airport-published planning guidelines, not a substitute for the actual check-in or bag-drop cutoff communicated by your airline. If you travel on hand luggage only with an online boarding pass, you may be able to go straight to security, but you should still allow the recommended time.

Families, passengers requiring additional assistance, and travellers with oversized baggage should check with their carrier before choosing transport. An overnight trip that looks perfect on paper may leave no buffer for early-morning queues or a missed coach departure.

Practical checklist before you book your overnight transfer

  • Confirm the flight time and airline on your booking (departure from London Stansted, airport code STN).
  • Set the airport target using Stansted's airline-specific check-in guidance.
  • Choose a service that actually arrives by that target. Don't confuse the first train from London with the first train leaving Stansted Airport.
  • Check the date carefully — a 01:00 coach for Monday morning runs after Sunday night, not on Monday night.
  • Check the stop and connection at the time you need to travel, including whether the Tube is running.
  • Keep a fallback such as an alternative coach route, licensed taxi or terminal-side hotel.
  • Recheck the evening before for timetable changes, roadworks, disruptions or airline messages.

Frequently asked questions about early Stansted flights

How do I get to Stansted Airport for a 5am flight?

For a 05:00 departure, Stansted's two-hour short-haul guideline means aiming to reach the terminal around 03:00; some airlines ask for three hours. Normal first trains from Liverpool Street are too late, so check a properly timed overnight coach, a pre-booked car or an airport hotel.

Can I get the first Stansted Express for a 6am flight?

Usually it is not a safe choice if you must reach check-in or security two or three hours before departure. The first Liverpool Street departures differ by day and even the earliest published regular service would not reach the airport by 04:00. Always confirm dated rail times.

Does National Express run to Stansted Airport overnight?

National Express advertises 24/7 London to Stansted services. However, the stop, route, departure and arrival times change by journey, so use its dated journey planner and choose a coach that reaches the terminal before your airline's check-in target.

What time does Stansted Airport open for early flights?

The airport says it operates around the clock, but the terminal closes between midnight and 02:00. It advises early departing passengers not to arrive before their scheduled check-in time and does not allow bedding down inside the terminal.

How early should I arrive for a Ryanair flight at Stansted?

Stansted's current check-in guidance says three hours before an international Ryanair flight and two hours for a Ryanair domestic flight. Confirm the exact instructions for your booking and check-in or bag-drop cutoff with Ryanair.

Is an airport hotel worth it before a 6am Stansted flight?

It may be worthwhile for travellers far from a night-coach stop, families, early check-in requirements or anyone who wants to avoid a long overnight transfer. Compare the room cost with the coach or taxi total and verify walking or shuttle arrangements.
