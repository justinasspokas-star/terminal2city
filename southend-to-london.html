Southend Airport to London by train: Stratford & Liverpool Street

Southend Airport has a major advantage for London travel: the railway station is beside the terminal and direct Greater Anglia trains run to Stratford and London Liverpool Street. If your hotel is in east London or the City, the airport-to-London train can be unusually straightforward.

Southend Airport to London train: quick answer

Is there a direct train from Southend Airport to London?

Yes. Greater Anglia runs direct trains from Southend Airport station to Stratford and London Liverpool Street. The station is beside the terminal, so you do not need an airport bus or shuttle to reach the railway.

Southend Airport → Stratford

Direct train. Official airport information publishes journeys around 43 minutes, while Greater Anglia describes the route at around 46 minutes depending on the service.

Southend Airport → Liverpool Street

Direct train to the City of London. Published references are roughly 52–55 minutes depending on the timetable.

Southend’s biggest advantage

The railway station is beside the airport terminal

Greater Anglia says Southend Airport station is about a two-minute walk from the terminal. Direct trains run to Stratford and London Liverpool Street, avoiding the airport-bus-to-station step found at some other airports.

Official sources currently describe journeys of roughly 43–46 minutes to Stratford and 52–55 minutes to Liverpool Street, depending on the service. Greater Anglia also says services to Liverpool Street can run up to three times per hour on weekdays.

Useful fare reference

Greater Anglia was showing fares from £13.40 each way when its route page was last updated on 21 August 2026. Treat that only as a dated reference and check the live fare.

Direct to the City

Southend Airport to London Liverpool Street by train

The train from Southend Airport to London Liverpool Street is direct, so there is no rail change on the airport leg. This makes it especially useful for hotels and offices around Liverpool Street, the City, Shoreditch and nearby east-central London.

Current official sources place the journey at roughly 52–55 minutes, depending on the service. Greater Anglia says weekday trains can run up to three times per hour, but frequencies and engineering work can change, so use the live journey planner for your date.

If your final destination is farther west or south, compare Liverpool Street with getting off earlier at Stratford. Stratford can be the better interchange for some east-London and Elizabeth line journeys.

Ticket & luggage tip

Greater Anglia currently shows fares from £13.40 each way on its Southend Airport → Liverpool Street page, based on a return fare. Treat this as a reference, not a guaranteed fare. With several bags, also check the final walk or onward connection from Liverpool Street.

Frequently asked questions

Southend Airport → London FAQ

Is there a direct train from Southend Airport to London?

Yes. Greater Anglia operates direct trains from Southend Airport to Stratford and London Liverpool Street.

How long does Southend Airport to London take by train?

Greater Anglia currently advertises about 46 minutes to Stratford and about 55 minutes to central London. Exact times vary by service and date.

How far is the station from Southend Airport terminal?

Greater Anglia says the airport railway station is about a two-minute walk from the terminal.

How frequent are trains from Southend Airport to Liverpool Street?

Greater Anglia currently states that trains run up to three times per hour on weekdays. Check the live journey planner for your travel date.

When is a private transfer worth comparing from Southend Airport?

It can be useful for groups, heavy luggage, late arrivals or destinations in west or south London where the direct train still leaves a substantial onward journey.
