Southend Airport to London: direct train or door-to-door transfer?

Southend is the furthest east of London’s main passenger airports, but it has one unusually useful advantage: the railway station is next to the terminal and direct trains run to Stratford and Liverpool Street.

Southend’s biggest advantage

The railway station is beside the airport terminal

Greater Anglia says Southend Airport station is about a two-minute walk from the terminal. Direct trains run to Stratford and London Liverpool Street, avoiding the airport-bus-to-station step found at some other airports.

Greater Anglia currently publishes reference journey times of about 46 minutes to Stratford and 55 minutes to central London. It also says services to Liverpool Street run up to three times per hour on weekdays.

Useful fare reference

Frequently asked questions

Southend Airport → London FAQ

Is there a direct train from Southend Airport to London?

Yes. Greater Anglia operates direct trains from Southend Airport to Stratford and London Liverpool Street.

How long does Southend Airport to London take by train?

Greater Anglia currently advertises about 46 minutes to Stratford and about 55 minutes to central London. Exact times vary by service and date.

How far is the station from Southend Airport terminal?

Greater Anglia says the airport railway station is about a two-minute walk from the terminal.

How frequent are trains from Southend Airport to Liverpool Street?

Greater Anglia currently states that trains run up to three times per hour on weekdays. Check the live journey planner for your travel date.

When is a private transfer worth comparing from Southend Airport?

It can be useful for groups, heavy luggage, late arrivals or destinations in west or south London where the direct train still leaves a substantial onward journey.
