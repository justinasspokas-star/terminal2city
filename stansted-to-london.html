The fastest simple rail option

Stansted Express goes direct to London Liverpool Street

The Stansted Express takes about 48 minutes to London Liverpool Street and about 36 minutes to Tottenham Hale. The operator states that trains run every 15 minutes during the day. The airport rail station is roughly a two-minute walk from the terminal.

That makes rail especially simple if Liverpool Street, the City, Shoreditch or an Underground/Elizabeth line connection works for your final address.

Do not stop at “London”

London is huge. A 48-minute train to Liverpool Street can still leave a long Underground or taxi leg if your hotel is in West London. Compare the final address before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Stansted Airport → London FAQ

How do I get from Stansted Airport to central London?

The main options are the direct Stansted Express train, scheduled coaches and taxi or private transfer. The right choice depends heavily on your final London address and arrival time.

How long is the Stansted Express to London?

The operator states about 48 minutes from Stansted Airport to London Liverpool Street and about 36 minutes to Tottenham Hale.

How often does the Stansted Express run?

Stansted Express states that trains run every 15 minutes during the day, although frequency and engineering work can vary. Check the live timetable for your date.

What time is the last train from Stansted Airport to London?

The current Stansted Express timetable lists the last airport departure around 00:30. First and last times vary by day and can change, so check again before travel.

Is a coach or private transfer better than the train?

It can be if its London stop is closer to your hotel, if you arrive outside rail hours, or if a group can split a fixed private-transfer fare. Compare door-to-door time rather than only the fastest rail segment.
