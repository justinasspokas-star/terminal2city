Which post-cruise tour should you book?

Choose the tour type according to your final destination, your available time and where your luggage will go. A sightseeing transfer ends somewhere different from its pickup point; a local tour may return you to Southampton. A shore excursion designed for passengers returning to a ship may not suit someone who has already disembarked.

Before booking: six practical questions

  1. Does the tour accept passengers from your specific ship and date?
  2. Can the operator collect you from your assigned terminal after disembarkation?
  3. Is the final drop-off your hotel, airport terminal or a central meeting point?
  4. Is there space for every suitcase, mobility aid and child seat you require?
  5. Are attraction tickets included, or do you need to book them separately?
  6. What happens if the ship is delayed or the itinerary changes?

If storing luggage separately, use our Southampton cruise luggage storage guide. Reserve enough time to collect it before a transfer. For a flight, use the Heathrow transfer guide to work backwards from check-in. No sightseeing itinerary is worth risking a missed departure.

Frequently asked questions

What is a post-cruise sightseeing transfer?

It combines a journey from the port to an onward destination with one or more attraction stops, unlike a standard point-to-point transfer.

Can I take my suitcases on a sightseeing transfer?

Check the specific product's luggage rules and vehicle capacity. Never assume all bags are accepted.

Can I book a Stonehenge tour after my cruise?

Selected Southampton tour and transfer listings include Stonehenge. Confirm dates, pickup eligibility and your final destination.

Are post-cruise tours suitable for an early flight?

Usually a direct transfer or an overnight airport stay is more sensible if the flight deadline is tight. Always check the actual schedule.
