A simple cruise journey checklist

  1. Confirm the ship, sailing date, terminal and embarkation or disembarkation arrangements.
  2. Check whether your transport starts at the ship, railway station, airport or hotel.
  3. Count large cases, mobility equipment and child seats before choosing a vehicle.
  4. Allow time for baggage collection and possible delays; do not plan around the ship's scheduled arrival alone.
  5. Confirm the provider's cancellation, delay and luggage policies before paying.

For an outbound cruise, arriving in the departure city a day early can reduce the risk of a missed sailing. If travelling independently on the same day, use your cruise line's actual boarding deadline.
