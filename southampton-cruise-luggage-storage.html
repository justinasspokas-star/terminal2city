Choose the best location for your onward journey

Leaving by train

Look near Southampton Central, especially if you plan to explore the city and return by rail. Check the walk to the platform and allow time to collect bags.

Leaving by coach

A location near the coach station can reduce your last-minute taxi journey. Confirm your departure stop and bag collection deadline.

Private transfer to the airport

Ask whether the driver can collect you at the storage address, or whether a dedicated pickup is required. If you plan a sightseeing transfer, ask whether bags can stay in the vehicle under the provider's terms.

Before embarkation

Storage can be useful if you arrive before the cruise check-in window. Confirm when you can drop bags at the terminal and how long it takes to return.

How much does Southampton left luggage cost?

Compare the total for all cases, not only the advertised starting rate. Some providers price per bag, per day or by size; others may add service or protection fees. Two suitcases at the published £15 walk-in rate would cost £30 for the day at Cruise Bag Drop. This is a worked example, not a live quote.

For a family, add the cost of taxis to and from the storage point. A cheaper provider further away may cost more overall.

Luggage storage checklist before you book

  • Check the actual opening and last collection times for your date, including Sundays and bank holidays.
  • Confirm suitcase dimensions, weight limits, oversize items and mobility aids.
  • Read protection limits, exclusions, identification rules and cancellation terms.
  • Keep passports, essential medication, valuables and travel documents with you.
  • Photograph and label bags; retain the booking receipt or claim tag.
  • Allow time to retrieve bags before your transfer, train or flight.

Do not assume that a cruise terminal or railway station has public lockers. Confirm a named storage provider in advance, particularly on busy turnaround days.

Can someone collect my luggage from the cruise terminal?

Some local services advertise terminal collection or onward delivery. Visit Southampton lists Burlington House as offering terminal drop-off and collection; confirm arrangements directly. Storage and delivery are different services: delivery requires an agreed handover point, recipient, time window and terms if the ship is late.

If you are flying the same day, only use an airport delivery arrangement that you have explicitly confirmed with the provider, including the exact airport terminal and latest handover time.

Frequently asked questions

Is there luggage storage at Southampton cruise port?

There are specialist providers near the port and in the city. Do not assume a public storage desk is available inside your assigned cruise terminal.

Can I store luggage near Southampton Central station?

Third-party storage networks list nearby locations. Check the actual address, opening hours and availability; this is not a claim that the station has its own lockers.

Can I leave my luggage for a few hours?

Many storage providers accept same-day bags. Check the minimum charge, collection deadline and whether your booking is flexible.

Is luggage insured?

Protection varies by provider, including limits and exclusions. Read the applicable terms and retain valuables and important documents yourself.
