The route most people should check first

Gatwick to Brighton has direct trains in about 30 minutes

London Gatwick says average direct rail time to Brighton is about 30 minutes, with services from Southern, Thameslink and Gatwick Express. Gatwick Airport station is at the South Terminal.

If you arrive at North Terminal, use the free terminal shuttle to South Terminal before heading to the rail station.

Important ticket detail

Gatwick Airport states that contactless payment is not valid for travel south of Gatwick to destinations such as Brighton. Buy a valid rail ticket rather than assuming London contactless rules continue to Brighton.

Frequently asked questions

Gatwick Airport → Brighton FAQ

Is there a direct train from Gatwick Airport to Brighton?

Yes. Gatwick Airport lists direct services to Brighton by Southern, Thameslink and Gatwick Express, with an average direct journey of about 30 minutes.

Where is the train station at Gatwick Airport?

The railway station is at South Terminal. If you arrive at North Terminal, Gatwick provides a free shuttle to South Terminal.

Can I use contactless from Gatwick to Brighton?

Gatwick Airport states that contactless payment is not valid for travel south of Gatwick to destinations such as Brighton. Purchase the correct ticket before travelling.

Is there a coach from Gatwick to Brighton?

Yes. Gatwick lists coach services to Brighton, including National Express and FlixBus. Journey time is traffic-dependent, so check the live service for your date.

Should I take a taxi or private transfer to Brighton?

It can make sense for groups, several suitcases, late arrivals or addresses outside central Brighton where a train would still require a local taxi or bus.
