Southampton cruise port guide: terminals, transfers and onward travel

Southampton has five cruise terminals across a working port. The correct terminal, your luggage and your onward timetable matter more than a generic port address. Use this guide to plan arrival, embarkation and departure.

Which Southampton cruise terminal do I need?

Check your booking documents and the cruise line's latest instructions before leaving for the port. Berth allocations and access arrangements may change. Do not rely on a previous sailing's terminal.

How to get to Southampton cruise port

From Heathrow Airport

Direct coaches and rail-based alternatives connect Heathrow with Southampton; a private transfer can take you to the ship. Compare Heathrow to Southampton transport.

From Gatwick Airport

Compare the coach, train itinerary and private transfer using your sailing's deadline. Compare Gatwick to Southampton transport.

From central London

South Western Railway operates London Waterloo–Southampton Central services. National Express also serves Southampton from London Victoria. Add the last-mile journey from your arrival station to the cruise terminal.

From Southampton Airport (SOU)

Southampton Airport Parkway is next to the airport; trains connect to Southampton Central. A taxi can be more practical for a direct terminal drop-off with several bags.

Southampton Central and coach station to cruise terminals

Southampton Central railway station and Southampton Coach Station are separate transport hubs, not cruise terminals. A short local taxi may be the easiest final connection, particularly for the eastern docks or for passengers with mobility needs. Walking may be practical for some western-dock journeys, but luggage, weather and port access can change that decision.

Book using the full cruise terminal name, not just “Southampton port”. For a private transfer, confirm meeting point, luggage capacity, child seats and how the driver handles delays.

What happens when you leave the ship?

Heading to Heathrow or London

Compare a direct coach, train plus airport connection or pre-booked private vehicle. Your pickup should allow time to collect luggage and clear the terminal.

Several hours before your flight

Need to leave your bags?

Staying overnight

Embarkation day: avoid unnecessary risks

Have your passport, boarding documents, medication and essentials in hand luggage. Follow the check-in window assigned by your cruise line. If arriving from abroad, an overnight stay before sailing may be preferable to relying on a tight same-day flight connection.

For independent travel, check flexible ticket conditions and the operator's disruption policy. A cheap airport ticket is not useful if it cannot get you to the ship before boarding closes.

Frequently asked questions

How many cruise terminals are in Southampton?

There are five main terminals: City, Horizon, Mayflower, Ocean and QEII.

Can I walk from Southampton Central to the cruise port?

Some western-dock destinations may be walkable. The actual terminal, permitted pedestrian route and your luggage determine whether walking is sensible.

Which airport is closest to Southampton cruise port?

Southampton Airport is the local airport. Heathrow and Gatwick are alternatives with wider flight networks; compare the full onward journey.
