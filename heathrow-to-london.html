The fastest is not always the shortest door to door

Paddington, the West End and the City need different answers

Heathrow Express is the fastest direct airport train to Paddington, with Heathrow currently publishing a 15-minute trip from Terminals 2 and 3. The Elizabeth line takes longer to Paddington but continues directly to Bond Street, Tottenham Court Road, Farringdon and Liverpool Street, which can remove a second transfer.

The Piccadilly line is slower but goes directly through central-London areas such as King’s Cross St Pancras, Leicester Square and Piccadilly Circus. That can be simpler than taking a faster train to the wrong side of central London.

Terminal reality

Heathrow Express, Elizabeth line and Piccadilly-line access differ slightly by terminal and service. Always check the live platform/service from your arrival terminal before leaving baggage reclaim.

Frequently asked questions

Heathrow Airport → London FAQ

What is the fastest way from Heathrow Airport to central London?

Heathrow Express is the fastest direct airport rail option to Paddington. Heathrow currently publishes a 15-minute journey from Terminals 2 and 3, with trains every 15 minutes.

Is the Elizabeth line direct from Heathrow to central London?

Yes. The Elizabeth line serves Heathrow Terminals 2 and 3, Terminal 4 and Terminal 5 and runs to central London stations including Paddington, Bond Street, Tottenham Court Road, Farringdon and Liverpool Street. Service patterns vary by terminal and train destination.

What is the cheapest rail option from Heathrow to London?

Heathrow describes the Piccadilly line as the most budget-friendly rail option. It serves all Heathrow terminals and reaches central London in roughly 50 minutes, depending on destination.

Should I take Heathrow Express or the Elizabeth line?

Choose by final destination. Heathrow Express is strongest for Paddington and speed; the Elizabeth line is often more convenient for Bond Street, Tottenham Court Road, Farringdon, Liverpool Street and onward east-London journeys.

When is a taxi or private transfer worth comparing?

A door-to-door vehicle can be useful for groups, several large bags, late arrivals or hotels that would otherwise require another London connection after the airport train.
