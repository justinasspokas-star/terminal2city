Train from Gatwick to London

Do not automatically buy Gatwick Express

Gatwick Express is the clear speed benchmark if you want London Victoria. London Gatwick currently publishes a 30-minute non-stop journey with two departures per hour. That is excellent for hotels around Victoria, Westminster, Pimlico, Belgravia and parts of the West End.

But a traveller staying near London Bridge or St Pancras can often avoid an unnecessary cross-London transfer by using Thameslink instead. Gatwick lists direct Thameslink services to London Bridge, London Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon and St Pancras International. In those cases, a train with a slightly longer airport leg can produce the shorter and easier door-to-door journey.

Southern also runs between Gatwick and London, including services to Victoria and Clapham Junction. This gives you another rail option when the timetable, fare or final destination fits better.

Terminal reality

Gatwick Airport railway station is at South Terminal. If you land at North Terminal, use the airport's free shuttle to South Terminal. Gatwick currently describes the shuttle ride as about two minutes.

Best option by destination

Best route from Gatwick Airport by London destination

Start with the station nearest your hotel. The fastest airport train is not always the fastest door-to-door journey.

Gatwick to Victoria / Westminster

Gatwick Express is the simplest speed benchmark. Also compare Southern services to Victoria if the timetable or fare suits you better.

Gatwick to London Bridge / South Bank

Thameslink to London Bridge can save a central-London interchange and put you close to many hotels south of the Thames.

Gatwick to Blackfriars / St Paul’s

Thameslink is especially useful because it runs through central London instead of stopping at Victoria.

Gatwick to Farringdon / Clerkenwell

A direct Thameslink train to Farringdon can be far easier than travelling to Victoria first and crossing London with luggage.

Gatwick to St Pancras / King’s Cross

Thameslink to St Pancras International is usually the rail option to check first for this part of London.

Gatwick to Clapham / south-west London

Southern via Clapham Junction can open useful onward connections without taking you unnecessarily into central London.

Tickets and payment

Can you use contactless from Gatwick to London?

Contactless and Oyster

London Gatwick states that you can use a contactless payment card or Oyster card for train travel between Gatwick and London by tapping in and out at the barriers. Each traveller who needs their own fare must follow the relevant payment rules.

This makes a flexible rail journey attractive after a flight because you are not necessarily tied to one exact departure.

Advance tickets and family savings

Gatwick says many rail operators offer cheaper fares when booked ahead. It also currently advertises a 10% online saving on Gatwick Express, Southern child tickets from £2 when travelling with an adult, and GroupSave savings for eligible groups of three or more.

Do not assume one ticket product is cheapest for every group. Compare the live total for your exact passengers and travel time.

Coach from Gatwick to London

Coach can be useful when price and luggage matter more than speed

National Express coaches serve London from Gatwick and depart from both North and South Terminals. Gatwick currently publishes an average journey of around 1 hour 45 minutes between Gatwick and London by National Express.

A coach can be attractive if you have large bags and prefer to put them in the luggage hold rather than move them through rail stations. It can also be worth checking when rail is disrupted. The trade-off is road traffic: a journey that looks acceptable on paper can become much slower at busy times.

After a delayed flight

Check the ticket's missed-departure and change rules before booking a fixed coach time. Flight arrival, passport control and baggage reclaim can make a very tight connection risky.

Taxi and private transfer

When a Gatwick Airport to London taxi can make sense

A taxi or private transfer from Gatwick to London is usually more expensive than public transport for one traveller, but the comparison changes for a group. Four people with several suitcases are comparing one vehicle price against four individual fares plus the effort of a final Tube, bus or taxi connection.

Door-to-door transport is particularly useful for young children, reduced mobility, very large luggage, hotels far from a direct Gatwick rail station or late-night arrivals. London Gatwick's official private-hire provider operates from both terminals, and other licensed operators also serve the airport.

Always compare the whole-group price, waiting-time policy, flight-delay policy and pickup instructions. Traffic on the M23 and into London means road journey times are less predictable than rail.

Do the group maths

For 3–5 travellers, compare the vehicle quote with the total rail or coach fare for everyone — then add any final London taxi or Tube leg. The cheapest individual ticket is not always the cheapest door-to-door group journey.

North Terminal vs South Terminal

Where do you catch the train or coach at Gatwick?

South Terminal arrivals

The railway station is directly connected to South Terminal. Gatwick says it is around a five-minute walk from departures and arrivals. Follow signs for the station after leaving baggage reclaim.

North Terminal arrivals

Use the free inter-terminal shuttle to South Terminal for trains. Coaches also serve North Terminal, so for a coach journey you may not need to transfer terminals. Check your operator's exact stop before leaving arrivals.

Practical decision

Gatwick Express vs Thameslink vs Southern

⚡Choose Gatwick Express when…

Victoria is genuinely useful for your hotel and you value the fastest non-stop airport train over finding the lowest possible rail fare.

🚆Choose Thameslink when…

London Bridge, Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon or St Pancras puts you closer to your final destination.

🔀Choose Southern when…

Victoria or Clapham Junction works well and the live timetable or fare fits your journey better than Gatwick Express.

Late arrivals and disruption

Landing late at Gatwick? Check the real terminal-exit time

A scheduled 22:30 landing is not the same as being ready to board a train at 22:30. Add time for taxiing, passport control, baggage reclaim and — for North Terminal passengers — the shuttle to the railway station.

Gatwick has expanded overnight public-transport options, but timetables, engineering work and disruption can change. For a late arrival, check the live train and coach departures on your exact travel date and keep a taxi or private-transfer backup in mind if the final connection to your hotel becomes awkward.

Rule of thumb

Do not book a plan that only works if your flight is perfectly on time. A slightly slower but flexible option can be safer after an international arrival.

Frequently asked questions

Gatwick Airport to London FAQ

What is the fastest way from Gatwick Airport to London?

Gatwick Express is the fastest non-stop rail option to London Victoria. Gatwick currently publishes a 30-minute journey and two departures per hour.

Which train should I take from Gatwick Airport to London?

Choose by final destination. Gatwick Express and Southern are useful for Victoria, while Thameslink runs directly to London Bridge, Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon and St Pancras International.

Where is the train station at Gatwick Airport?

The railway station is at South Terminal. North Terminal passengers can use the free inter-terminal shuttle, which Gatwick says takes about two minutes.

Can I use contactless or Oyster from Gatwick to London?

Yes. Gatwick states that contactless and Oyster can be used for rail travel between Gatwick Airport and London. Tap in and out correctly and follow the rules for each passenger.

Is there a coach from Gatwick Airport to London?

Yes. National Express serves London from both Gatwick terminals. Gatwick publishes an average coach journey of about 1 hour 45 minutes, but traffic can change the real time.

Is Gatwick Express better than Thameslink?

Not universally. Gatwick Express is strongest for Victoria and non-stop speed. Thameslink is often more practical for London Bridge, Blackfriars, Farringdon and St Pancras because it can remove a London interchange.

When is a taxi or private transfer worth comparing?

Compare a vehicle for larger groups, several suitcases, young children, reduced mobility, late arrivals or a hotel that is awkward from the direct Gatwick rail stations.
