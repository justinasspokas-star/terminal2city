London City Airport to central London & Canary Wharf

London City is different from the other London airports: the DLR station is at the airport and you are already in east London. The useful question is not “how do I reach London?” but which DLR or Underground connection gets closest to your real address.

The big advantage

The DLR station is at London City Airport

TfL lists London City Airport as a DLR station, with services towards Bank and Stratford International among the departures. TfL also states that all DLR stations have step-free access from street to train.

That makes London City unusually straightforward with luggage: there is no separate airport rail shuttle before you enter the London transport network.

Canary Wharf is close, but not the same line

For Canary Wharf, compare a DLR + Underground/DLR interchange rather than assuming every airport train runs directly there. London City Airport currently gives about 25 minutes as a reference journey time to Canary Wharf.

Where the DLR helps most

London City Airport publishes reference times of about 35 minutes to Shoreditch, 44 minutes to Covent Garden, 45 minutes to Westminster and Piccadilly, and 50 minutes to Oxford Circus. Treat these as planning references and check TfL live status for your travel time.

Bank / City of London

Start with the direct DLR towards Bank. It avoids an Underground change before reaching the City.

Canary Wharf

DLR plus a short network interchange is usually the public-transport benchmark; compare it with a taxi if several people are travelling together.

Westminster / West End

Expect at least one interchange. Count the final walk from the Tube station to your hotel.

Stratford / east London

Direct DLR services towards Stratford International can be especially useful when your destination is east rather than central London.

Frequently asked questions

London City Airport → London FAQ

How do I get from London City Airport to central London?

London City Airport has its own DLR station. Direct DLR services run towards Bank, while other central London areas are reached with an interchange to the Underground or Elizabeth line depending on the destination.

How do I get from London City Airport to Canary Wharf?

A common public-transport route uses the DLR from the airport and an interchange towards Canary Wharf. London City Airport currently publishes about 25 minutes to Canary Wharf as a reference journey time.

Is the DLR station at London City Airport step-free?

TfL states that all DLR stations have step-free access from street to train, which is useful for luggage and pushchairs.

Can I travel directly from London City Airport to Bank?

Yes. TfL lists direct DLR services from London City Airport towards Bank.

When is a taxi or private transfer worth considering?

It can make sense for several travellers, heavy luggage, late arrivals or a final address that would otherwise require multiple public-transport legs.
