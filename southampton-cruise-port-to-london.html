How to get from Southampton cruise port to London

South Western Railway runs trains from Southampton Central to London Waterloo. National Express offers coaches from Southampton Coach Station to London Victoria. Neither station is the same as your cruise terminal: plan the short initial transfer and the final journey to your hotel.

Choose your London arrival point before booking

Waterloo can be convenient for the South Bank, while Victoria may suit travellers staying near Victoria or Westminster. If your accommodation is elsewhere, compare the entire journey, including any Underground, bus or taxi changes. Station choice should depend on the hotel postcode, walking distance and luggage, not just train speed.

Hotel check-in is later

Ask your hotel whether it can hold luggage before check-in. Otherwise compare storage near the arrival station. Don't assume a sightseeing coach will keep bags after your tour ends.

How to compare London transfer prices for your party

Multiply train or coach fares by the number of passengers and include both station transfers. Compare that total with a private vehicle quote. If considering sightseeing, include admission where it is not bundled and the value of a separate attraction visit. Prices and travel times vary with dates, traffic and rail disruption, so check live offers instead of treating estimates as bookings.

With children or mobility needs, confirm ticket age rules, accessible vehicle arrangements and the number of luggage pieces before purchasing.

Frequently asked questions

Which station do trains from Southampton arrive at in London?

Direct South Western Railway services from Southampton Central run to London Waterloo; check the timetable for the specific service.

Can I book a transfer straight to my London hotel?

Some private transfers offer hotel drop-off. Confirm the exact address, vehicle size and pickup arrangements.

Can I visit Stonehenge on the way to London?

Yes, sightseeing transfer listings include Stonehenge on selected itineraries. Confirm eligibility for your ship and hotel drop-off before booking.

Is the coach or train cheaper?

There is no universal answer. Compare date-specific fares and all local transport costs for your entire party.
