Which London airport is best? Compare all six before you book the flight

The best airport for London is not always the one with the cheapest airfare or the shortest train time. Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, London City and Southend connect to different parts of the city. The right choice depends on your hotel, landing time, luggage and the true cost of getting there.

Quick answer

Choose the airport that fits your London destination — not the airport name

Heathrow is the strongest all-rounder. London City is excellent for Canary Wharf and the City. Gatwick is very strong for Victoria and the Thameslink corridor. Stansted naturally fits Liverpool Street and Tottenham Hale. Luton is strong for St Pancras and Thameslink destinations. Southend can work very well for Stratford and Liverpool Street when the flight itself is attractive.

1. Best London airport for Paddington and west-central London: Heathrow

If your hotel is near Paddington, Bayswater or west-central London, Heathrow is difficult to beat. Heathrow Express reaches Paddington in about 15 minutes from Terminals 2 and 3, while the Elizabeth line also serves Paddington and continues through central London.

Heathrow also has the Piccadilly line, which gives it a second lower-cost direct rail corridor through the West End. That combination of fast rail, cross-London rail and Underground access is why Heathrow is usually the most flexible all-round London airport.

Strong Heathrow case

Your flight price is competitive, you are staying in west or central London, or you value having several public-transport backups after landing.

2. Best airport for Victoria — and a very strong option for the Thameslink corridor: Gatwick

Gatwick Express runs non-stop to London Victoria in around 30 minutes. But Gatwick is much more than one express train: Southern and Thameslink give the airport direct rail links to multiple London stations. Gatwick says trains reach London in roughly 30 to 45 minutes depending on the service and destination.

This makes Gatwick particularly interesting if your hotel is near Victoria, London Bridge, Blackfriars, Farringdon or St Pancras. The correct question is not “Is Heathrow or Gatwick better?” but “Which airport gives me the cleanest route to my hotel?”

If you land at Gatwick North Terminal, remember that the railway station is at South Terminal. The airport's free terminal shuttle takes about two minutes, so include that step in the real journey.

3. Best airport for Liverpool Street and parts of east/north London: Stansted can make sense

The Stansted Express reaches Liverpool Street in about 48 minutes and Tottenham Hale in roughly 36 minutes. The station is only a short walk from the terminal, which makes the airport leg easy to understand.

For a hotel around Liverpool Street, Shoreditch or areas well connected from Tottenham Hale, Stansted can be much more sensible than its distance from central London suggests. But if your hotel is in west London, the cheap flight may come with a long cross-city journey after the airport train.

This is a good example of why the phrase “best London airport for central London” is too broad. Central London is not one transport destination.

4. Best airport for St Pancras and some north-central / Thameslink destinations: Luton

Luton Airport connects to Luton Airport Parkway using the Luton DART, then EMR and Thameslink connect directly to London. London Luton Airport publishes journeys from St Pancras to the terminal in as little as 32 minutes, including the DART connection.

Thameslink also serves Farringdon, City Thameslink, Blackfriars and London Bridge. If your hotel is close to those stations, Luton can be a much better fit than its reputation as a “far away budget airport” suggests.

One ticket detail matters: when buying rail travel, check whether you are selecting Luton Airport rather than only Luton Airport Parkway if you expect the DART portion to be included. The fare structure can differ.

5. Closest London airport for Canary Wharf and the City: London City

London City Airport is the most central of the six and is only about five miles from the City of London. Its DLR station connects directly into the TfL network. The airport publishes indicative journeys of around 25 minutes to Canary Wharf, 35 minutes to the Tower Bridge area and 45 minutes to Westminster.

If your airline serves London City and the airfare is reasonable, it can be extremely attractive for Canary Wharf, the City, Docklands and short business trips. Its weakness is not ground transport — it is the smaller choice of flights compared with Heathrow or Gatwick.

6. Do not ignore Southend if you are staying in east London

Southend is the smallest of the six main London passenger airports, but its station is almost beside the terminal. Direct Greater Anglia trains reach Stratford in about 43 minutes and Liverpool Street in about 52 minutes.

For Stratford, the City or east London, a good Southend airfare can therefore be more practical than people assume. For west London, however, the onward cross-London journey can erase much of that advantage.

Heathrow vs Gatwick: which one should you choose?

Heathrow vs Gatwick is the comparison most visitors make, but there is no universal winner.

✈️Heathrow is often better if…

Your hotel is near Paddington, the West End or an Elizabeth-line station; you want several public transport options; or the flight choice is significantly better.

🚆Gatwick is often better if…

Your hotel is near Victoria or a direct Thameslink station such as London Bridge, Blackfriars, Farringdon or St Pancras.

💷Let the total cost decide if…

The flights differ significantly in price. Add the whole group's airport transfer cost before assuming the cheaper airfare is the cheaper trip.

The biggest mistake: comparing airfare without the airport transfer

A £35 cheaper flight is not automatically £35 cheaper. You need to compare:

  • airfare for every traveller
  • airport-to-hotel transport for the whole group
  • extra cost for children or special ticket rules
  • final taxi or Tube connection
  • time lost getting across London
  • late-night backup if the flight is delayed

For a one-night or two-night London trip, an extra hour in each direction can be more important than a modest airfare saving. For a family of four, ground-transport cost can also change the ranking.

Families and luggage can change the answer

Two adults travelling with hand luggage can tolerate a different journey from two adults, two children, a stroller and four suitcases. The cheapest train on paper can become a poor fit if it adds two London interchanges and a 15-minute walk to the hotel.

That is why Terminal2City treats group size, children's ages and large bags as decision inputs rather than afterthoughts. A direct rail service plus a short final taxi can sometimes be the best-value compromise.

Late arrival? The “best airport” can change after 23:00

A great daytime rail connection can become less attractive after a late flight. Last trains, engineering works and reduced frequencies matter, especially at airports farther from your hotel.

If two flights are similarly priced, a 15:00 arrival and a 23:30 arrival should not necessarily produce the same airport choice. Compare the expected terminal-exit time, not only the scheduled landing time.

Frequently asked questions

Which London airport is best for central London?

It depends on the part of central London. Heathrow is the strongest all-rounder, London City is excellent for the City and Canary Wharf, Gatwick is strong for Victoria and Thameslink destinations, Stansted is strong for Liverpool Street, and Luton fits St Pancras and the Thameslink corridor well.

Is Heathrow or Gatwick better for London?

Heathrow offers more direct transport types and is excellent for west/central London. Gatwick can be better for Victoria, London Bridge, Blackfriars, Farringdon and St Pancras. Compare the hotel location and total trip price.

Which London airport is closest to the city centre?

London City Airport is the closest of the six main passenger airports to central London and is particularly convenient for east London, Canary Wharf and the City.

What is the best London airport for a cheap flight?

The cheapest airfare is not automatically the cheapest trip. Stansted, Luton, Gatwick or Southend may offer a lower fare, but add the full ground-transfer cost and time before deciding.

Which London airport is best with lots of luggage?

Prioritise a direct route to the area where you are staying. A slightly slower train with no London interchange can be easier than the fastest airport train followed by multiple changes.
