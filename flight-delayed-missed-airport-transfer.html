Flight delayed and missed your airport transfer? Here's what to do

Your flight arrives late, the booked coach has left and you still need to get into London. Before buying another ticket, check the fare you already have: a later train may be valid, a coach add-on may protect you, and a private driver may have a flight-delay policy.

The short answer

A late flight does not always mean losing your airport transfer ticket

First, check your booking. Heathrow Express and Stansted Express tickets can be valid for other trains on the same booked day. National Express has a paid Change & Go option for eligible coach fares. Pre-booked taxis and private transfers depend on the company's own flight-tracking, waiting-time and no-show terms. The most expensive mistake is paying again before checking what you already purchased.

Missed your airport transfer? Follow these six steps

  1. Check the actual arrival time. Use your airline's flight status and distinguish touchdown from the time you can leave the terminal after passport control and baggage reclaim.
  2. Open the original confirmation. Find the operator, fare type, booked date and time, booking reference and any flexible-travel add-on.
  3. Contact the operator before departure if you still can. Coach ticket amendments may have a deadline. For a private car, message or call your driver using the booking details rather than assuming they know about the delay.
  4. Check whether a later service is valid. Some airport rail tickets are valid on any train on the date shown; a fixed coach seat may not be.
  5. Compare the next practical route. Check the latest operating times, arrival terminal, actual destination, group size and luggage before buying a replacement.
  6. Keep evidence. Save the flight delay notice, old ticket, conversations with the operator, alternative ticket and receipts in case your policy or booking protection accepts a claim.

Missed a National Express coach because your flight was delayed?

National Express explains that if you miss a coach, a normal single ticket is no longer valid and a new ticket is required. But there are important exceptions to check before you buy again. On eligible Standard and Fully Flexible fares, the Change & Go add-on allows a change to another available departure within 12 hours before or after the scheduled coach, subject to seat availability and applicable conditions. National Express lists the add-on at £5 per adult, each way, when added during online booking; check the current price and eligibility before paying.

If you did not buy Change & Go, use Manage My Booking immediately if your coach has not departed. Fully Flexible and Standard fares have different amendment terms; Restricted fares are generally non-amendable. Do not assume a driver can accept an invalid ticket on a later coach, even if seats appear free.

Example: delayed at Stansted

You booked a 15:00 coach and are still at passport control at 15:10. Check Change & Go on the original booking first. If it applies, look for the next available coach. If you bought a Restricted ticket without the add-on, expect to purchase a replacement unless the operator confirms an exception.

What if you miss your airport train?

Heathrow Express: check the travel date, not only the time

Heathrow Express says its Advance Discounted Single is valid on any Heathrow Express train on the travel date printed on the ticket, despite the word “Advance”. It is non-refundable and non-amendable. Its standard single tickets also offer same-day flexibility. This is especially helpful if your flight lands a few hours late, but a delay past midnight may create a different ticket-validity problem. Compare Heathrow Express with the Elizabeth line before deciding whether a separate ticket is necessary.

Stansted Express: its Advance ticket is not the usual train-specific Advance fare

Stansted Express publishes that both its Anytime and its own Advance tickets allow you to board any Stansted Express train on the booked outbound date. That does not guarantee use on the next calendar day. If a flight cancellation or rescheduling changes your travel date, check whether a temporary flight-disruption policy applies before paying for another ticket.

Gatwick Express and other National Rail tickets

Gatwick Express Anytime tickets allow travel on the date and selected route, subject to operator restrictions, but other fare types may not. A separate missed flight is not the same as a cancelled train: Delay Repay normally concerns delays to the rail service itself, not an airline arriving late. Check the specific ticket rules or ask station staff before boarding.

Elizabeth line: useful when you do not want a fixed departure

At Heathrow, the Elizabeth line supports pay as you go with Oyster or contactless, so there is no booked train departure to lose. If you pay contactless, tap in and out using the same card or device. Check the last train and your onward connection, especially for late-night arrivals.

Your next move at each London airport

There is no single best backup. A late coach at Heathrow may leave rail options; a very late Stansted arrival may require checking coach services and last departures. Verify current timetables, then compare the airport-to-final-address journey rather than the fastest first leg.

Can you claim money back from the airline, transfer company or insurance?

Do not assume your airline must reimburse an independently booked airport coach or train ticket. UK flight-delay passenger rights can provide care and, in some cases, compensation when the airline is responsible for an eligible delay. But those rights do not automatically make the airline liable for every separately booked ground-transfer cost. Airline compensation is also different from a refund of your unused ground-transport ticket.

  • Coach or rail operator: ask whether your ticket can be amended, reused or refunded under its own fare conditions. Train delay compensation usually requires a delay to the train service.
  • Airline: check the UK Civil Aviation Authority's passenger guidance for your flight. If the flight was part of a package, notify the package organiser as well.
  • Travel insurance: inspect the wording for missed departure, travel disruption or additional transport expenses. Policy exclusions, excesses and proof requirements vary.
  • Private transfer: request the operator's written flight-delay and no-show policy. Some drivers track incoming flights, but waiting and rebooking are not universal.

When contacting any provider, send the booking reference, scheduled and actual arrival times, explanation of the delay, your unused ticket and receipts for replacement transport. Ask what specific clause applies; do not pay claim intermediaries unless you understand their fees.

How to avoid this problem next time

  • Book with the landing-to-pickup gap in mind. Touchdown, disembarkation, passport control, baggage reclaim and walking all take time. Use our Heathrow arrivals-time calculator to check a realistic pickup buffer.
  • For coaches, compare the flexibility cost with the risk of rebuying. A cheaper Restricted fare can be poor value when flight timing is uncertain.
  • For rail, read the date restriction. “Advance” means different things on different airport rail operators.
  • For private transfers, confirm whether the driver tracks the flight. Ask when the waiting-time clock starts and what happens after the included grace period.
  • Plan the late-arrival fallback before flying. Save the operator's contact details and one backup journey. Check live services on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if my flight is delayed and I miss my National Express coach?

For an ordinary missed single ticket, National Express says a new ticket is usually needed. Check whether you purchased the Change & Go add-on or can amend an eligible fare before its booked departure.

Can I use a later Heathrow Express train if my flight is delayed?

Usually yes on the same travel date for its standard and discounted advance single tickets. Do not assume that a ticket valid on Tuesday will work on Wednesday if the delay takes you past midnight.

Can I use my Stansted Express ticket after a flight delay?

Stansted Express Anytime and its own Advance tickets allow any train on the booked outbound date. If your journey shifts to a different calendar date, check the operator's current disruption policy.

Will my airline refund a missed airport transfer?

Not automatically. It depends on whether the ground transport formed part of your booking, the circumstances of the flight disruption and any additional contractual or insurance protections.

What if my flight arrives after the last airport train?

Check official live coach services and any remaining local transport first. Compare the complete cost and arrival time of coach, night transport and taxi to your address; do not assume there will be an overnight train.
