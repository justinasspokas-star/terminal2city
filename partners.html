Partner with Terminal2City

We are building a focused comparison platform for direct airport ground transport in England. Our initial goal is simple: show travellers current route options clearly, then send them to the transport operator to complete the booking.

What we are looking for

We are currently selecting launch partners rather than claiming a complete live marketplace.

Live fares

API, booking feed or authorised pricing endpoint that lets us display current route fares accurately.

Timetables & availability

Departure/arrival times and, where available, seat or journey availability for selected dates.

Tracked booking path

Route-specific deeplinks, reseller booking flow or another authorised handoff into operator checkout.

✓
Affiliate or reseller commission
Commercial terms on completed bookings.
✓
Net fares
Where supported, we are open to a net-rate distribution model.
✓
Brand & fare display permission
Clear rules for displaying operator name, logo, fares and journey data.
✓
England airport routes
Initial focus: all six main London passenger airports, then wider England.
